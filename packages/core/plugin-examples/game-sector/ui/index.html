How to play

R B E A
Build a Refinery, Barracks, Reactor or App. Then click and hold to spend.
T F H G
Test Turret, Feature, Archive, Plugin Dock, as your rank unlocks them
C
Connect two buildings. Only connected buildings get power and work.
Z X V J
Train a Scout, Constructor, Warden or Judge at the Barracks
Right-click
Order selected agents: carry, build, fight, repair, move
Drag
Select several agents. Shift+1 to 5 saves a group, 1 to 5 picks it.
Tab · Enter
Jump to the next decision · approve it
Arrows · wheel
Move and zoom the map. Middle-drag or the minimap also move it.
Space
